About the Company

A Merchant Exporter Dealing Fly Ash in Bulk

Jay Jagannath Marine Eximss was founded in 2017 by Abinash Rout, who brought more than a decade of experience in a family business spanning mining, transport and fuel retail. Today JJME exports fly ash and zinc ash to international markets, and trades minerals, ore, marine products, fruits and vegetables within India.

Our Mission

To redefine global trade through excellence, innovation and sustainability — connecting the world with seamless and ethical international transactions.

Our Vision

A future where international trade is a harmonious blend of innovation, ethical practice and global connectivity.

Technical Reference

Class F and Class C Fly Ash

PropertyClass FClass C
Coal sourceOlder, harder coals — anthracite or bituminous.Younger, softer coals — lignite or sub-bituminous.
Calcium contentLow, generally below 10% CaO.High, typically above 20% CaO, containing free lime.
CompositionRich in silica and alumina.Contains free lime alongside silica and alumina.
ReactivityPozzolanic — requires a cementing agent such as Portland cement to react and harden.Both pozzolanic and self-cementing.
Best useStructural concrete in coastal or sulphate-rich environments; resists expansion and chemical attack.Rapid construction, road base stabilisation and roller-compacted concrete, for early strength development.
