The Shape of the Market

Three Neighbours, 98% of the Volume

India generated 340 million tonnes of fly ash in FY 2024–25 and utilised 97.8% of it. The export share is concentrated: Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh absorb almost everything that leaves the country, feeding cement plants and infrastructure projects across the border.

Demand beyond that belt is growing — the Gulf and Southeast Asia are the markets opening up fastest for Indian ash.
