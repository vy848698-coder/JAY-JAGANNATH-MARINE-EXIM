Trade Terms

Which Incoterm Suits You

We quote on any of the four below. Select one to see where our responsibility ends and yours begins.

Free On Board — we deliver the cargo on board the vessel at the named port of shipment. Sea freight and onward risk are yours.

We cover

  • Packing and marking
  • Inland haulage to the port
  • Export clearance and shipping bill
  • Loading on board

You cover

  • Sea freight
  • Marine insurance
  • Discharge at destination
  • Import duty and clearance

Risk passes to you once the goods are loaded on board at the port of shipment.

Cost and Freight — we pay the freight through to your named destination port, but insurance remains yours to arrange.

We cover

  • Everything under FOB
  • Sea freight to the destination port

You cover

  • Marine insurance
  • Discharge at destination
  • Import duty and clearance

Cost and risk split here: we pay the freight, but risk still passes to you on loading at the port of shipment.

Cost, Insurance and Freight — as CFR, and we also take out marine insurance in your favour for the voyage.

We cover

  • Everything under CFR
  • Marine insurance, minimum 110% of contract value

You cover

  • Discharge at destination
  • Import duty and clearance

Insurance under CIF is minimum cover, Institute Cargo Clauses (C). Ask for wider cover if your bank or contract requires it.

Ex Works — we make the packed goods available at our premises and you take over from that point. Minimum obligation on our side.

We cover

  • Packing and marking
  • Making goods available for collection

You cover

  • Loading and inland haulage
  • Export clearance
  • Freight and insurance
  • Import duty and clearance

Rarely the best choice for a first-time overseas buyer — export clearance from India falls to you.

Paperwork

The Document Set

Released against your bank's terms, with the shipment tracked through to your discharge port.

Commercial InvoiceCarries our IEC and GSTIN, the 8-digit HS code and the agreed currency.
Packing ListBag counts, net and gross weights, marks and container or hold detail.
Shipping BillThe customs declaration filed to obtain the Let Export Order.
Bill of LadingThe carrier's receipt, contract of carriage and document of title.
Certificate of OriginProof of Indian origin, used for preferential duty where an FTA applies.
Test CertificateLot results against ASTM C618, BS EN 450 S or IS 3812.

Your buyer, bank or destination customs may call for further documents — inspection certificates, insurance certificates or a letter of credit set. Tell us early and we prepare them with the shipment.
