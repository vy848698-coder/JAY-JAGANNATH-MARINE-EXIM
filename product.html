The Material

What Is Fly Ash?

Fly ash is the fine, powder-like residue left when pulverised coal is burned in a thermal power station. It is a grey amorphous powder, rich in silica and alumina and spherical in shape.

Its properties vary with the coal burned, the efficiency of combustion and the selection process — which is why we classify every consignment rather than ship it as raw ash.

Two Classified Grades

Class F and Class C Fly Ash

The class depends on the coal burned and the chemistry that results. Both are stocked for export.

Export · Low Calcium

Class F Fly Ash

Source
Burning older, harder coals — anthracite or bituminous.
Properties
Low in calcium, generally below 10% CaO, and rich in silica and alumina. Pozzolanic: it needs a cementing agent such as Portland cement to react and harden.
Best Use
Structural concrete in coastal or sulphate-rich environments, where it resists expansion and chemical attack.
Standard
ASTM C618 Class F · BS EN 450 S · IS 3812
Export · High Calcium

Class C Fly Ash

Source
Burning younger, softer coals — lignite or sub-bituminous.
Properties
High in calcium, typically above 20% CaO, and contains free lime. Unlike Class F it is both pozzolanic and self-cementing.
Best Use
Rapid construction, road base stabilisation and roller-compacted concrete, for early strength development.
Standard

Also Supplied

Bottom Ash, Pond Ash and Zinc Ash

Domestic · Coarse Grade

Bottom Ash

The coarse, heavier particles collected at the bottom of the boiler furnace. Higher in unburnt carbon, and it possesses no pozzolanic property — so we supply it as a separate grade.

Domestic · Recovered

Pond Ash

Fly ash and bottom ash carried as slurry to the ash dyke and recovered from there for bulk civil work.

  • Land fill
  • Mine fill
  • Road laying
Export · Byproduct

Zinc Ash

A powdery byproduct formed on the surface of molten zinc during hot-dip galvanisation and metal smelting — metallic zinc particles, zinc oxide and trace impurities.

  • Zinc sulphate and fertilisers
  • Zinc oxide production
  • Metal recycling and recovery
