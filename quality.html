Our fly ash complies with the stringent international codes for classified ash. Every lot is drawn, tested and certified before it is released — the certificate travels with the shipment.

Quality Analysis

The parameters reported on a fly ash test certificate, the limit each code sets, and the method used to establish it.

Loss on ignition 6.0% max (ASTM C618) · 5.0% max (IS 3812, EN cat. A) Ignition, ASTM C311
Sulphur trioxide (SO₃) 5.0% max (ASTM C618) · 3.0% max (IS 3812) Chemical analysis, ASTM C311
Strength activity index 75% of control min (ASTM C618) ASTM C311, at 7 or 28 days
Lime reactivity Reported per lot IS 1727 — 1 : 1 : 9 pozzolan, lime, sand; 50 mm cubes, 8 days at 50 °C
Soundness Reported per lot Autoclave expansion, ASTM C311 / IS 1727
Total chlorides 0.05% max (IS 3812) · 0.10% max (EN 450-1) Chemical analysis

Meeting either the 7-day or the 28-day strength activity index satisfies ASTM C618 — a lot that is slow at 7 days is not rejected if it reaches 75% of control by 28. Limits above are the published figures of each code, given for orientation; the governing document is the test certificate issued against your consignment.

Before Despatch

How a Lot Is Certified

01

Sample

Samples are drawn from the classified lot, not from a nominal stockpile, so the certificate describes the material you actually receive.

02

Test

Fineness, chemistry and reactivity are established against ASTM C618, BS EN 450 S or IS 3812, whichever your specification calls for.

03

Certify

The test certificate is issued for the lot and travels with the shipping documents. Pre-contract samples are available on request.
